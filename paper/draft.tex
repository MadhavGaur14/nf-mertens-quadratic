\title{The na\"ive Mertens-type conjecture fails for $\Q(\sqrt{-3})$ and $\Q(\sqrt5)$}
\author{Madhav Gaur}
\date{\today}

\begin{document}

\begin{abstract}
Hu, Kaneko, Martin and Schildkraut introduced a number field analogue of the Mertens conjecture and proved that it is
false for every quadratic field other than $\Q(\sqrt{-3})$ and $\Q(\sqrt5)$. We settle these two remaining cases. By
a computer-assisted proof in rigorous interval arithmetic, we show that the normalized Mertens function
$x^{-1/2}M_K(x)$ has $\limsup>2.6830$ and $\liminf<-2.7849$ for $K=\Q(\sqrt{-3})$, and $\limsup>1.7645$ and
$\liminf<-1.7550$ for $K=\Q(\sqrt5)$, unconditionally. Consequently the na\"ive Mertens-type conjecture fails for every
quadratic field. We also give certified replacements for three numerical witnesses in the earlier work that we were
unable to reproduce.
\end{abstract}
